Import a BOM

Upload a CSV or XLSX from LCSC, DigiKey, KiCad, or any spreadsheet. The importer auto-detects the supplier and columns.

📦 Order BOM adds stock

An order you placed. Matching parts get restocked; new parts are added to inventory with their specs filled in.

🔧 PCB BOM uses stock

The parts a board needs. We check what you have, flag any shortages, then deduct the parts when you build it.

Tip: column names don't have to match exactly — the importer recognizes common variants (Qty / Order Qty / Quantity, MPN / Manufacturer Part Number, LCSC Part Number / Customer NO., Designator / Reference, and more).
